iPhone Keynote 2018 - the Highlights

Thousands of excited tech-fans flocked to Cupertino, California on Wednesday September 12th in order to get a sneak peek at the newest products which will soon be available. The event took place in the Steve Jobs Theater at Apple HQ and the announcements were widely met with enthusiasm. Fans were excited to hear that 3 new phones and the Apple Watch 4 would be hitting the market in the coming months. Just in time for Christmas of course!

iPhone Xs

The iPhone Xs uses the same super retina display that was so popular on the iPhone X. This OLED technology is able to capture colors and contrast with more accuracy than any previous display. The screen is slightly bigger than that of the iPhone X at 5.8 inches. The phone is also installed with state of the art processing technology. The A12 Bionic chip is capable of performing 5 trillion operations per second. Coincidentally, the A11 chip was only able to perform 600 billion operations per second. In addition, the image capturing quality of the Xs has been improved with smarter HDR technology. This will reduce the amount of blur on photos even when they are taken at high speeds. Apple has also announced that the battery life of an Xs will be around 30 minutes longer than its predecessor.

Features:

● Six-core CPU

● Four-core GPU

● IP68 waterproof rating

● Storage from 64GB to 512GB

● 7 MP front camera

● 12 MP dual Camera

Available for shipping now

RRP: $999

iPhone Xs Max

The Xs Max boasts the largest screen out of any apple phone in history. At 6.5 inches this device will give users an incredibly satisfying viewing experience using the latest display technology. Much like the Xs, it has super retina display and the latest in OLED technology. Watching videos on the go has never been so immersive!

Features:

● Six-core CPU

● Four-core GPU

● IP68 waterproof rating

● Storage from 64GB to 512GB

● 7 MP front camera

● 12 MP dual Camera

Available for shipping now

RRP: $1099

iPhone XR

The introduction of the iPhone XR was welcoming news for iPhone lovers looking for an upgrade without breaking the bank. It has been compared to the iPhone 5c due to its smaller display and slightly downsized features. It comes in a variety of different colours and has been tipped as a product at the top of most children’s Christmas lists this Winter.

Features:

● Six-core CPU

● Four-core GPU

● IP68 waterproof rating

● Storage from 16GB to 512GB

● 5 MP front camera

● 9 MP dual Camera

Available for shipping from October

RRP: $749

Apple Watch 4

Announcement of the new Apple Watch will be mellifluous to many. One of the main complaints about the Apple Watch 3 was issues with cellular connection and the size of the display. Apple have confronted both these problems and guaranteed a new, flawless version.

Available for shipping now:

RRP $499

After the Keynote conference many have already begun drafting their Christmas Lists!
