Device Condition

You will get the most cash if your phone is in mint condition but you will still do well if it’s in good condition. Most used cell phones fall under the “good” condition category, if you are unsure of the condition of your phone this is a good place to start. As long as your cell phone does not have a cracked screen or back, all buttons, mics, and speakers are working well, the phone holds its charge and turns on, your phone will be considered “good” condition.

Like New:

Your item is in Like New condition if it is in 100% perfect condition like new out of the box, no scratches or scuffs. Battery health must show above 90% for iPhones under Battery Health in the settings

Good Condition:

Good condition is the most common device condition. Average wear & tear consistent with normal use but 100% fully functional. No water damage.

What if your iPhone is damaged?

Don’t worry you can still sell a damaged iPhone. If it has a cracked or broken screen don’t bother to repair before selling it’s not worth it, plus cheap parts can ruin your phone.

Minor Damage

Minor damage is one of any of the following: cracked screen with touch response and no dark spots or dead pixels, cracked camera, bad button, or bad headphone jack. If your items have more than one these issues it will be considered as damaged. Any items with water damage will be considered DAMAGED even if functioning.

Damaged

Any device that is damaged with more than one issue from the minor damage category, water damage, bent and/or dented housing, cracked LCD with no display.
