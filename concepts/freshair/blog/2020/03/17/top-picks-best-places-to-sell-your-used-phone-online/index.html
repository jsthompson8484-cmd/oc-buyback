Top Picks: Best Places To Sell Your Used Phone Online

Have an old phone that is a few years old, in decent condition, but you are not sure what to do with it? One option is to give your old cell phone back to your service provider through a trade in program and get a new phone.

Another option to consider is selling your old phone instead as you may get a better value. You can pocket the extra cash and put it towards anything you like.

Several online companies are in the business of buying used phones for cash. Below are our top picks on where to sell your used phone from the comfort of your home.

1. OCBuyBack.com

OCBuyBack.com is an online buyback site based out of Southern California that pays out cash for phones, tablets, iPods, and more. This buyback company takes the hassle out of selling your old phone by selling it for you in exchange for cash.

The journey to getting paid is fairly simple. On the OCBuyBack website, you will first click on “Get Your Cash”, where you will then select the device you are selling and answer a series of questions on the condition of your used cell phone. You will then be offered an immediate quote.

For a Samsung Galaxy S10 (128GB) in good condition, you can get paid $315 or for an iPhone 11 Pro (256GB) in good condition you can get $565. OCBuyBack provides a free shipping label during checkout and you are able to track your shipment throughout the entire process. Once your used phone is received, OCBuyBack will pay you within 24 hours by check or PayPay (Locals can be paid cash in-store).

2. Gazelle.com

Gazelle.com is an online reCommerce company that buys and sells used electronics including phones, iPads, Macbooks, and more. Gazelle resells your old cell phone so you don’t have to.

To get started, you will select the type of device and model of your used phone. After answering a few questions on the condition of your phone, you will receive an immediate offer.

For a Samsung Galaxy S10 (128GB) in good condition, they offer $191. For an iPhone 11 Pro (256GB) in good condition, they offer $498.

Gazelle offers free shipping on items worth $1 or more. Once your old phone is received, they pay out in the form of Amazon gift card, PayPal, or check within a few business days.

3. Swappa.com

Swappa.com is a self-described “friendly marketplace” where you can sell and buy used phones, tablets, laptops, and more.

To get started, you first select the brand and model of your used cell phone. You will then be asked to create an account so you can create a listing. You can provide a few photos and a brief description detailing the condition of your used phone.

With Swappa, you are able to set your own price and through a messaging system, communicate with potential buyers to get the best cash offer. Once sold, you will be paid through PayPal.

Do note you are responsible for shipping the phone to the buyer yourself.

4. uSell.com

uSell.com is an online third-party channel that offers a simple selling process and allows you to compare prices between buyers.

After selecting the cell phone you would like to sell, you can select between Damaged, Good, or Flawless to best describe the condition of your used phone. For a Samsung Galaxy S10 (128GB) in good condition, they offer $280. uSell does not currently offer a selling option for an iPhone 11 Pro.

uSell.com provides a free shipping label as well as tracking so you can check on the status of your phone while in transit. Once your used phone is received, USell pays out by PayPal or check within 5 business days.

There are numerous ways you can sell your old phone for cash with hardly ever having to leave your home. Perhaps you want to be part of the global recycling movement or you simply need some extra pocket cash. Either way, by selling your old phone to one of the companies listed above, you will be able to achieve both goals with ease.
